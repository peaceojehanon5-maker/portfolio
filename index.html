// about

A career built where infrastructure meets people

My work has always come back to one thing: keeping systems — and the people who depend on them — connected. I started out crimping cables and splicing fibre at a Cisco Networking Academy in Benin City, then spent three years in the network operations centre of one of Africa's largest solar-powered ISPs, configuring BGP sessions and bringing new base stations online for a network serving millions.

Since moving to the UK I've completed an MSc in Big Data Technologies while working front-line support roles — which means I've also been the calm voice at 2am when something broke. Along the way I've reconciled millions in revenue records, built data pipelines on Google Cloud, and written more setup guides and runbooks than I can count.

I'm drawn to problems that sit between networks, data and cloud — and I try to leave everything better documented than I found it.
